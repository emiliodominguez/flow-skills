# Direction archetypes

Starting points, not templates. Each lists what it's good for, typical tokens, and how it fails.
Fonts are examples of a category; use what the project licenses and can load.

## Calm product

- **For:** SaaS apps, dashboards, settings, anything used daily.
- **Tokens:** one neutral grotesk (variable if available), scale ratio about 1.2, 4px spacing grid,
  small radii (4-8px), hairline borders over shadows, one accent used only for primary actions and focus.
- **Dials:** density 5-7, variance 2-4, motion 2-4.
- **Fails when:** everything is gray with no hierarchy. Fix it with type weight and spacing, not more color.

## Editorial

- **For:** content-led marketing, publishing, portfolios, brand stories.
- **Tokens:** serif or high-contrast display face paired with a quiet sans for body, scale ratio
  1.25-1.414, generous whitespace, strong grid with deliberate breaks, restrained palette with one
  ink-like accent.
- **Dials:** density 2-4, variance 5-8, motion 3-5.
- **Fails when:** display type wraps into 5+ lines on mobile, or body text gets too light and small to read.

## Swiss and minimal

- **For:** tools, documentation, design-savvy audiences, anything that should feel precise.
- **Tokens:** one grotesk in two or three weights, strict column grid, flush-left text, black and
  white plus one signal color, almost no shadows, square or small radii.
- **Dials:** density 4-6, variance 3-5, motion 1-3.
- **Fails when:** it gets so sparse that affordances disappear. Controls still need to look like controls.

## Soft and tactile

- **For:** consumer, health, education, onboarding-heavy products.
- **Tokens:** rounded humanist or geometric sans, larger radii (12-24px) with concentric inner radii,
  warm tinted neutrals, soft diffuse shadows with a single light direction, friendly illustration.
- **Dials:** density 2-4, variance 3-5, motion 4-6.
- **Fails when:** low-contrast pastels break text contrast, or everything floats with no grounding.

## Technical and dense

- **For:** developer tools, analytics, trading, operations consoles.
- **Tokens:** sans plus mono, tabular numbers, compact controls (28-32px rows), dividers instead of
  cards, dark and light themes with equal care, status colors with non-color indicators.
- **Dials:** density 7-10, variance 1-3, motion 1-3.
- **Fails when:** density turns into clutter. Group with whitespace and alignment, not boxes.

## Expressive and bold

- **For:** launches, campaigns, entertainment, youth brands.
- **Tokens:** a characterful display face, large scale jumps (ratio 1.5+), saturated accent with
  a tight supporting palette, overlapping or broken-grid composition, choreographed motion.
- **Dials:** density 1-3, variance 7-10, motion 6-9.
- **Fails when:** it ignores reduced motion, hides content behind animation, or costs seconds of load time.

## Industrial and utilitarian

- **For:** manufacturing, logistics, archives, deliberately raw brands.
- **Tokens:** condensed grotesk or mono headings, rigid grid, visible structure lines, a
  near-monochrome palette with one hazard or signal color, no rounded corners.
- **Dials:** density 6-8, variance 4-6, motion 1-3.
- **Fails when:** tiny uppercase labels with wide tracking hurt legibility; keep label text at least 12px.
