## [ed-adversarial-review](skills/ed-adversarial-review.md)

Use for anything security-, money-, data-, auth-, or concurrency-sensitive, after /ed-review, or when invoked as /ed-adversarial-review (also "red team this", "how could this break", "attack this", "poke holes in the code", "what could go wrong in prod"). Complements /ed-review (which asks "is it correct?"); this asks "how do I break it?". Hands off to /ed-work to fix, then re-run. Invoke as /ed-adversarial-review in Claude Code or $ed-adversarial-review in Codex.
